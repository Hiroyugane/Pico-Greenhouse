Anchors are physical values: at 0 = far too low (deviation 0), ideal = deviation 50, at 100 = far too high (deviation 100). They define what each deviation means for this species — no surface reads °C or %RH, so editing them moves the axis values, the tooltips and the cell detail, not the plotted duty. The effective anchor is night + b × (day − night).

Tuning

Paste over the matching keys in config.py. Surface blocks are emitted as a full _surface(...) call listing every non-default parameter, and profile blocks as the whole species entry, so both replace what is there outright. A changed surface also needs the golden vectors regenerated — python prototypes/plot_regulation_surfaces.py — or the surface tests will fail; profile, schedule and adapter edits do not touch them.

Surface grids are baked from lib/regulation_surface.evaluate at generate time and re-evaluated live in-browser by a JS port of the same maths — the banner above proves the two agree. Fonts fall back to the system stack (offline file, no webfonts).
