Paste over the matching keys in config.py. Surface blocks are emitted as a full _surface(...) call listing every non-default parameter, so they replace the existing call outright. Then regenerate the golden vectors — python prototypes/plot_regulation_surfaces.py — or the surface tests will fail.

Surface grids are baked from lib/regulation_surface.evaluate at generate time and re-evaluated live in-browser by a JS port of the same maths — the banner above proves the two agree. Fonts fall back to the system stack (offline file, no webfonts).
